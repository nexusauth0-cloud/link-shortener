# Monetization — Nexus Links

> Pricing philosophy, plan structure, and growth strategy.

## Pricing Philosophy

1. **Transparency.** All prices public. No "contact sales" gates on pricing page.
2. **Usage-based ceilings, not metering.** Once you hit your plan limit, you upgrade — you don't get surprise bills.
3. **Value-based, not feature-based.** Price correlates with the value the customer gets (clicks managed, team members, data retention).
4. **Free tier must deliver genuine value.** Not a trial — a forever-free plan that's genuinely useful for solo creators.

## Plan Psychology

### Free ($0)

**Goal:** Acquire users who will upgrade when they hit limits.

The Free plan is genuinely useful for solo creators — 100 active links, 1K clicks/month, 1 custom domain. Users hit limits gradually:

- More links → need Pro
- More clicks → need Pro
- Team collaboration → need Business

### Pro ($29/mo)

**Goal:** Individual professionals and small teams.

The "no-brainer" plan for anyone using Nexus Links professionally. 10K links and 100K clicks is enough for most small businesses. API access unlocks developer use cases.

### Business ($79/mo)

**Goal:** The core revenue driver.

Unlimited links, 1M clicks, 20 team members, priority support. This is where most B2B SaaS revenue comes from. The jump from Pro ($29) to Business ($79) is deliberate — wide enough to make Pro seem affordable, narrow enough that Business feels worth it.

### Enterprise (Custom)

**Goal:** Large organizations with compliance needs.

Custom pricing for SSO, audit logs, dedicated support, and custom SLAs. Typically $500–$2,000/mo depending on volume and requirements.

## Billing Philosophy

- **Annual billing:** 2 months free (effectively 17% discount)
- **No setup fees** — never charge to start
- **Prorated upgrades** — upgrade mid-cycle, pay the difference
- **No prorated downgrades** — downgrade takes effect at next billing
- **30-day money-back guarantee** — no questions asked (reduces purchase anxiety)
- **Invoicing for Enterprise** — NET-30 for annual contracts

## Growth Strategy

### Self-Serve (70% of revenue)

- Free → Pro upgrade from dashboard (usage limit nudges)
- Pro → Business upgrade from dashboard (team size nudges)
- No sales involvement below $500/mo

### Sales-Assisted (20% of revenue)

- Business → Enterprise upgrades
- Custom onboarding and migration support
- Demo used as qualification tool

### Partner (10% of revenue)

- Agency reseller program (wholesale pricing)
- Referral program (20% of first year revenue)
- Integration partnerships
