# Marketing — Nexus Links

> Positioning, launch strategy, and growth channels.

## Positioning

**For teams who manage links at scale** (developers, marketing agencies, e-commerce brands)
**Who need** intelligent link management with real-time analytics and API-first workflows
**Nexus Links** is an enterprise-grade link platform
**Unlike** Bitly and Rebrandly
**Nexus Links** is built for the way modern teams work — API-first, AI-powered, with transparent pricing and a developer experience that doesn't sacrifice marketing power.

### Elevator Pitch

> "Nexus Links turns every link you share into a source of intelligence — without the enterprise overhead or the opaque pricing."

## Launch Plan

### Phase 1: Pre-Launch (4 weeks before)

- [ ] Landing page with email capture
- [ ] Waitlist signup with referral tracking
- [ ] Social media presence (X/Twitter, LinkedIn)
- [ ] Founders begin posting daily (building in public)
- [ ] Draft Product Hunt listing
- [ ] Reach out to 20 beta testers

### Phase 2: Launch (Week 0)

- [ ] Product Hunt launch (Tuesday)
- [ ] Hacker News Show HN post
- [ ] X/Twitter launch thread
- [ ] LinkedIn launch post
- [ ] Email launch announcement (waitlist)
- [ ] Launch blog post
- [ ] Partner co-marketing (if any)

### Phase 3: Post-Launch (Weeks 1-4)

- [ ] Respond to all Product Hunt comments
- [ ] Write 2-3 blog posts about lessons learned
- [ ] Case study with first 5 customers
- [ ] Fix critical launch feedback
- [ ] Begin paid acquisition (low-budget test)

## Social Strategy

### X/Twitter

- **Account:** `@nexuslinks`
- **Content:** Building in public, feature previews, developer tips
- **Frequency:** 3-5 posts per day
- **Style:** Authentic, technical, humble

### LinkedIn

- **Account:** Company page + founder profiles
- **Content:** Thought leadership, marketing tips, case studies
- **Frequency:** 2-3 posts per week
- **Style:** Professional, valuable, shareable

### GitHub

- **Account:** `nexuslinks` organization
- **Content:** Open-source SDKs, community tools
- **Frequency:** As needed (releases, issues)

## Email Strategy

### Transactional

- Welcome email (on signup)
- Milestone emails (100th click, 1000th click)
- Weekly digest (top links, top campaigns)
- Invoice receipts
- Account security alerts

### Marketing

- Monthly newsletter (product updates, tips)
- Feature announcement emails
- Upgrade nudges (hitting plan limits)
- Re-engagement (inactive for 30 days)

## Referral Program

- Referrer gets 20% of referred customer's first year revenue
- Referee gets 1 month free on any paid plan
- Tracked via unique referral link (shortened, naturally)
- Paid out as account credit
- No cap on referrals
